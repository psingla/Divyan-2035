# Learning in the AI Era

Learning is an active process: explore, question, build, test, and reflect.

## Principles

- **Follow curiosity.** Start with questions that matter to you.
- **Learn by doing.** Make small things and test your understanding.
- **Treat failures as evidence.** Record what broke and what you learned.
- **Use AI as a partner.** Ask for explanations and feedback, then verify the results.
- **Keep your own judgment.** Understand and take responsibility for what you create.
- **Reflect regularly.** Capture discoveries and decide what to try next.

## A Simple Learning Loop

1. Ask a question.
2. Explore possible answers.
3. Build a small experiment.
4. Test and observe.
5. Record what you learned.
6. Repeat with a better question.
