# JEE 2028 Study Guide

A working reference for Divyan's JEE preparation alongside ALLEN coaching.
Use this guide to choose the next study action, not to collect more resources.

## Folder Structure

- [Physics](physics/): concept notes, derivations, and practice.
- [Mathematics](mathematics/): concept notes and worked problems.
- [Chemistry](chemistry/): Physical, Organic, and Inorganic study notes.
- [Tests](tests/): mock-test reviews, error logs, and retry tracking.

Keep topic-specific notes, revision, and ALLEN assignments in the subject
folder, and test analysis in tests. Shared resource links and cross-subject
study guidance stay in this guide. Avoid duplicate copies of material.
Do not commit private contact details, roll numbers, or student records.

## Core Study Loop

1. Attend the ALLEN lesson and note anything unclear.
2. Revise the concepts the same day; explain them without looking at notes.
3. Attempt assigned problems independently before reading solutions.
4. Take unresolved doubts to the teacher or academic coordinator.
5. Practise relevant previous-year questions (PYQs) once the basics are secure.
6. Review errors and retry missed questions without the solution.

Keep ALLEN coursework as the main track. Add a reference book or video only
when it addresses a specific gap.

## Subject Checklists

### Physics

- Understand what each formula means, its assumptions, and when it applies.
- Draw diagrams and check units, signs, and limiting cases.
- Use H. C. Verma for concept-building and selected practice where needed;
  avoid duplicating every exercise across several books.
- Move from basic questions to coaching exercises and then PYQs.
- Write short concept notes and practise important derivations.
- Use videos to resolve a doubt, then solve a related question unaided.

### Chemistry

- Read NCERT carefully, especially for Inorganic Chemistry.
- Test recall rather than relying only on repeated reading.
- Revise reactions with their conditions and exceptions.
- Practise Physical Chemistry numericals and understand Organic Chemistry
  reasoning rather than memorizing disconnected answers.
- Cover weak areas as well as strengths; do not ignore an entire branch.
- Check coverage against the official syllabus for the exam year when available.

### Mathematics

- Secure algebra, trigonometry, and basic calculus before advanced applications.
- Practise a manageable set of problems with full written reasoning.
- Revisit questions that required hints or solutions.
- Use timed mixed-topic practice to check both accuracy and topic selection.
- Use test results to prioritize revision, not fixed chapter-weightage claims.

## Habits Observed Among JEE Toppers

These user-shared observations are anecdotal, not a guarantee of rank or a
personality checklist. Use them to build sustainable habits, not to judge
Divyan against an idealized topper.

| Shared observation | Practical habit to try |
| --- | --- |
| Punctuality and near-complete attendance | Attend consistently and arrive prepared; catch up after illness or unavoidable absence. |
| Staying up to date with the syllabus | Track pending lessons and assignments, and address backlog early. |
| Listening carefully and implementing teacher advice | Note feedback, clarify it if needed, and apply relevant advice in the next practice session. |
| Making few mistakes and rarely repeating them | Record why errors happened and retry similar questions to check the correction. |
| Attempting difficult problems independently | Make a genuine attempt before asking for help; set a time limit so one problem does not consume the session. |
| Asking for a hint rather than a full solution | Request the smallest useful hint, try again, and then explain the completed solution yourself. |
| Being humble and competitive | Learn from stronger performances and compete with your own previous accuracy and preparation, not with a need to defeat a peer. |
| Rarely needing external motivation | Build a repeatable routine; asking for encouragement or support is normal. |
| Staying focused despite peer pressure | Protect planned study time while retaining sleep, exercise, friendships, and reasonable breaks. |
| Remaining honest even when others cheat | Attempt tests honestly so scores reveal real strengths and gaps. |
| Taking a practical approach to exam answers | Use valid elimination, estimation, symmetry, or option substitution when appropriate; respect exam rules and the marking scheme. |

In practice, understand the full reasoning. In a timed exam, choose an
efficient valid method rather than insisting on the longest derivation.
"By hook or by crook" must not mean cheating or unsupported guessing.

### Small Weekly Habit Check

- Did I keep up with lessons, or make a clear catch-up plan?
- Did I apply one piece of teacher feedback?
- Did I attempt challenging questions before seeking hints?
- Did I retry a previous mistake successfully?
- Did I study and test honestly while protecting rest and health?

Choose one habit to improve next week rather than trying to perfect all of
them at once.

## Weekly Review

- Which concepts can I now explain without notes?
- Which assigned questions or chapters are still pending?
- Which mistakes recur: concept, calculation, reading, or time management?
- Which doubts should I ask at ALLEN?
- What should I revise next week based on evidence from practice and tests?
