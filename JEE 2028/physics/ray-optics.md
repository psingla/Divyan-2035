## How to Use

1. Pick a specific doubt from an ALLEN lesson or practice question.
2. Watch the relevant explanation and draw the ray diagrams yourself.
3. Note the sign convention, formula assumptions, and conditions of use.
4. Attempt related ALLEN problems without the video or solution.
5. Practise relevant PYQs and record any recurring mistakes.

Use these resources to supplement coaching, not to create a duplicate course
backlog.

## Understanding Checks
